\section*{0. Formal statements}

Standalone files state the main claims with as little project code as possible; each is paired
with a proof file that imports the development.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Conway's refinement conjecture using the surreal numbers from
    \href{https://github.com/vihdzp/combinatorial-games}{CombinatorialGames};
  \item the same conjecture over a
    \href{https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4}{Mathlib}-only construction of the
    needed surreal numbers, order, and arithmetic;
  \item Mathlib-only statements of the main results on generalised power series.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{0.1 Conway's cut formulation}

The concise
\blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/CombinatorialGames/ConwayRefinement.lean}
  {CombinatorialGames statement}
uses Conway's equation
\[
  x=\{x-1\mid x+1\}
\]
to define omnific integers.  If $a,b,c,d$ satisfy this equation and $ab=cd$, then there are
omnific integers $e,f,g,h$ such that
\[
  a=ef,\qquad b=gh,\qquad c=eg,\qquad d=fh.
\]

\subsection*{0.2 Conway's refinement conjecture from first principles}

The
\blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/Mathlib/InlineConwayRefinement.lean}
  {Mathlib-only statement}
constructs well-founded games, Conway's arithmetic and order, numeric games, surreal-number
representatives, the omnific-integer cut, and the same four-factor proposition in one file.

\subsection*{0.3 Generalised power series}

The following files import only Mathlib.  Throughout, $K$ is a field of characteristic zero.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/Mathlib/CompleteHahnGerm.lean}
    {Germs over a Cauchy-complete group}: if $G$ is a nontrivial ordered abelian group that is
    Cauchy complete for the group uniformity of its order topology, and $J$ is the ideal of
    series in $K((G^{\le0}))$ whose support is bounded away from $0$, then $K((G^{\le0}))/J$ is
    isomorphic as a $K$-algebra to a polynomial ring over $K$, $J$ is prime, and every equation
    $ab=cd$ of germs has a refinement.
  \item \blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/Mathlib/GermPolynomialRing.lean}
    {Berarducci's ring of germs}: for the ideal $J$ of $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))$ generated by
    the monomials $t^x$, $x<0$, the ring $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))/J$ is isomorphic to a polynomial
    ring over $K$ and is a unique factorisation domain.
  \item \blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/Mathlib/HahnSeriesPolynomialRing.lean}
    {Polynomial presentation of $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))$}: $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))$ is
    isomorphic to a polynomial ring over its subring of series with finite support.
  \item \blueprintfilelink{ConwayRefinement/Standalone/Mathlib/HahnSeriesGCD.lean}
    {Greatest common divisors in $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))$}: $K((\mathbb R^{\le0}))$ is a GCD
    domain, every series is primal, every irreducible series is prime, and two factorisations
    into irreducibles agree up to order and units.
\end{itemize}
